            Apex · Async
          

          
Flex Queue Dispatcher

          
An async job dispatcher that queues work beyond the Apex flex queue limit and feeds it to the platform as slots free up — so batch-heavy orgs stop failing with "too many jobs".

          
    
            
  • custom job queue + scheduler
    • 
            
  • priority & retry handling
    • 
            
  • observability of pending work

            Apex · Integrations
          

          
Bulk Email Relay

          
A 100% backend relay that routes Apex-originated email through an external provider, so Sales Cloud orgs can send at volume without Marketing Cloud. UI sends stay native and keep the org's email limit intact.

          
    
            
  • provider-agnostic adapter layer
    • 
            
  • only Apex sends are relayed
    • 
            
  • no UI, no extra licenses
